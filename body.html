The Black Knights ID // ZERO-01

Lelouch vi Britannia

Alias: Lelouch Lamperouge / Zero

▾ Click card to toggle classified dossier

The Eleventh Prince of the Holy Britannian Empire and son of Emperor Charles zi Britannia. After being exiled to Japan as a political hostage, Lelouch vows to obliterate Britannia and build a gentler world for his blind and crippled sister, Nunnally. Granted the "Power of Absolute Obedience" by C.C., he dons the mask of Zero and commands the Black Knights with genius-level tactical precision.

  • Ability: Geass of Absolute Obedience
  • Knightmare Frame: Type-0/0A Shinkiro / Gawain

Geass Directorate ID // CODE-CC

C.C. (Pronounced "C2")

Alias: The Immortal Witch

▾ Click card to toggle classified dossier

An immortal young woman bearing the Code of Geass, making her impervious to aging and fatal wounds. Having lived for centuries and witnessed the rise and fall of countlessGeass users, she forms a contract with Lelouch to save his life in Shinjuku. Beneath her aloof demeanor and obsession with Pizza Hut lies a tragic past and an unwavering loyalty as Lelouch's closest accomplice.

  • Ability: Code of Immortality / Geass Bestowal

Knights of the Round ID // Z-07

Suzaku Kururugi

Alias: The White Reaper / Knight of Zero

▾ Click card to toggle classified dossier

Son of Japan's last Prime Minister and Lelouch's childhood best friend. Unlike Lelouch, who believes the ends justify the means, Suzaku joins the Britannian military as an Honorary Britannian hoping to reform the corrupt empire peacefully from within. As the pilot of the experimental Lancelot, his unmatched physical reflexes and tragic "Live On" Geass command make him Zero's deadliest rival and ultimate partner.

  • Condition: Bound by Lelouch's "Live!" Geass Command
  • Knightmare Frame: Z-01 Lancelot Albion
  • Allegiance: Holy Britannian Empire / Zero Requiem

Revolution vs. Reform: The Clash of Ideologies

Directed by Gorō Taniguchi and written by Ichirō Ōkouchi with original character designs by CLAMP, Code Geass: Lelouch of the Rebellion examines whether a corrupt world should be shattered from the outside through revolution (Lelouch) or changed gradually from within by following the rules (Suzaku).
